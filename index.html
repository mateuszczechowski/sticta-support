Help for Sticta on macOS

Sticta turns your own photos into stickers, lets you arrange them on a scene (a travel map, a fridge door or a free-form background) and sets the result as your desktop wallpaper.

Need help? Write to sticta@proton.me. We read every message and usually reply within a few working days.

Please include your macOS version, your Mac model, the Sticta version (Sticta → About Sticta) and, if you can, a screenshot of the problem.

Frequently asked questions

My wallpaper didn't change after I edited a project.

Sticta never changes your wallpaper on its own. After editing, click the button that applies the project to your desktop. The same applies when you connect or disconnect a display: the button shows that the wallpaper is out of date and waits for you.

Can I use a different project on each display?

Yes. Open Displays in Sticta and assign a project to each connected screen. The assignment is remembered.

Why wasn't my photo placed on the map automatically?

Automatic placement uses the location saved in the photo (GPS data). Photos without location data, or with the location removed, can still be placed by hand by dragging the sticker onto the right country.

Sticta is taking up a lot of disk space.

Each sticker keeps its source photo so you can edit it later. Open Settings → Storage to see how much space Sticta uses and to clean up files that are no longer needed. You can also delete stickers you no longer use from the sticker bank.

Does Sticta upload my photos anywhere?

No. Sticta works entirely on your Mac and doesn't connect to the internet. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I get rid of the menu bar icon?

Open Settings → General and turn off the menu bar icon.
