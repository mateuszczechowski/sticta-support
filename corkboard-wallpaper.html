How to make a corkboard photo wallpaper for your Mac

In short: Choose the Corkboard style on the Canvas and drop in your photos. Each one is held by a pushpin and sits a little askew, like a real board in a hallway.

01

What you end up with

A cork board with a wooden frame in place of your desktop, covered with photos held by coloured pushpins. Cut-outs and prints overlap and sit slightly crooked, because a real board is never tidy.

02

Make it in Sticta

  1. Create a project and open the Canvas.
  2. Pick the Corkboard style. Change the look with Cork tone and Frame color.
  3. Drag your photos in. Each sticker gets a pushpin.
  4. Drag stickers around, make them bigger or smaller and let some overlap. There is no grid on a corkboard.
  5. Click Set as Wallpaper.
03

Mix cut-outs and prints

Want a different look? Select a sticker and pick a style in the inspector: Classic (cut-out with an outline), Polaroid, Original photo (the photo as it was taken, no cut-out) or Clean cut. Mixing styles makes a wallpaper feel less uniform.

On a corkboard, Original photo looks like a print pinned to the board and a cut-out like a sticker. The example mixes about half and half.

04

Add a clothesline

A clothesline works on a corkboard too: switch on Clothesline in the canvas settings and hang a few photos from it between the pinned ones.

05

Private by design

Sticta never connects to the internet and your photos never leave your Mac. Privacy policy.
