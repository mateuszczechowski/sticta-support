+My wallpaper didn't change after I edited a project.

Sticta never changes your wallpaper on its own. When you're happy with your edits, click the button that sets the project as your wallpaper. The same goes for connecting or disconnecting a display: the button shows the wallpaper is out of date and waits for you.

+Can I use a different project on each display?

Yes. Open Displays in Sticta and assign a project to each connected screen. Sticta remembers the assignment.

+Why wasn't my photo placed on the map automatically?

Automatic placement uses the location saved in the photo (GPS data). If a photo has no location data, or it has been removed, you can still drag its sticker onto the right country yourself.

+Sticta is using a lot of disk space.

Each sticker keeps its original photo so you can edit it later. Open Settings → Storage to see how much space Sticta uses and to clean up files nothing uses anymore. You can also delete stickers you no longer need from the sticker bank.

+Does Sticta upload my photos anywhere?

No. Sticta runs entirely on your Mac and never connects to the internet. Read the privacy policy for the details.

+How do I hide the menu bar icon?

Open Settings → General and turn off the menu bar icon. You can turn it back on at any time.
