Wallpapers on Apple TV

Sticta 1.1

Apple TV can't use a wallpaper directly.

Apple doesn't let apps set the Apple TV's background, or add their own screen saver. What it does allow is a screen saver that shows an album from iCloud Photos, so Sticta puts your wallpapers in a "Sticta" album there.

  1. On your Mac, choose File → Export Wallpaper… (⌘E).
  2. Tick your projects and the 4K TV or 1080p TV size.
  3. Tick Add to Photos and allow Sticta to use your photo library when macOS asks.
  4. On your Apple TV, open Settings → General → Screen Saver → Memories & Slideshows, and choose the "Sticta" album.

iCloud Photos must be turned on, on both your Mac and your Apple TV, with the same Apple ID. The photos can take a few minutes to arrive.

Changed a project? Export it again. Each export adds a new photo to the album; tick Replace earlier versions to have Sticta remove the older ones (macOS asks once to confirm). Nothing else in your library is touched, and deleting a project in Sticta never removes photos from Photos.

Photos access turned off? Sticta shows a card in the export window with a button that opens System Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos; allow Sticta there and come back. Prefer not to give access? Tick Save as files instead, then drag the exported PNGs into an album in the Photos app and use that album for the screen saver.

+My wallpaper didn't change after I edited a project.

Sticta never changes your wallpaper on its own. When you're happy with your edits, click the button that sets the project as your wallpaper. The same goes for connecting or disconnecting a display: the button shows the wallpaper is out of date and waits for you.

+Can I use a different project on each display?

Yes. Open Displays in Sticta and assign a project to each connected screen. Sticta remembers the assignment.

+Why wasn't my photo placed on the map automatically?

Automatic placement uses the location saved in the photo (GPS data). If a photo has no location data, or it has been removed, you can still drag its sticker onto the right country yourself.

+Sticta is using a lot of disk space.

Each sticker keeps its original photo so you can edit it later. Open Settings → Storage to see how much space Sticta uses and to clean up files nothing uses anymore. You can also delete stickers you no longer need from the sticker bank.

+Does Sticta upload my photos anywhere?

No. Sticta runs entirely on your Mac and never connects to the internet. Read the privacy policy for the details.

+How do I hide the menu bar icon?

Open Settings → General and turn off the menu bar icon. You can turn it back on at any time.

+Can I print a wallpaper?

Yes. In Export Wallpaper… (⌘E), the Print sizes (10 × 15 cm, 13 × 18 cm, 20 × 25 cm, A4 and A3, or 4 × 6, 5 × 7 and 8 × 10 inches) save landscape PNG files at 300 DPI, so Preview and your printer know the paper size. They are saved as files only, not to Photos. If a project's photos are small for the paper, Sticta adds a "May print soft" note under it; you can still export.

+Can I use my Sticta wallpaper on Apple TV?

Yes, through Photos: export it with Add to Photos, then pick the "Sticta" album as your Apple TV screen saver. Step by step.
