The beta has ended. Thank you!

In short: Sticta is out now on the Mac App Store. Install it from there; it replaces the beta and keeps your projects and stickers.

Download on the Mac App Store
01

Switching from the beta

Install Sticta from the Mac App Store. Your projects and stickers stay where they are. You can then remove Sticta from TestFlight. The beta build stops working once it expires.

02

Test purchases don't carry over

Sticta Unlimited bought in the beta was a free test purchase, so it doesn't unlock the App Store version. Buy it there, or redeem a code if you have one.

Beta giveaway
