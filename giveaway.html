Beta giveaway rules

In short: the 10 beta testers who send the most useful feedback get Sticta Unlimited free. The beta ended when Sticta launched on 7 October 2026; we're picking the winners now.

01

Organiser

The giveaway is run by Mateusz Czechowski, the developer of Sticta (“we”). Contact: sticta@proton.me. Apple is not a sponsor of this giveaway and is not involved in it in any way.

02

Who can take part

Anyone aged 18 or over who joins the Sticta beta on TestFlight. You don't need to be on the Sticta email list. Taking part is free: no purchase, rating, review, share or follow is needed, and none of these improves your chances.

03

How to take part

Send us feedback about Sticta while the beta runs, through the TestFlight feedback button or by email to sticta@proton.me. Bug reports, things that confused you, ideas and wallpapers you made all count. You can send as much feedback as you like. So we can reach you if you win, put your email address in at least one piece of TestFlight feedback, or send your feedback by email.

04

When

From the day the beta opens until the day Sticta launches on the Mac App Store. We announce the launch date on sticta.app and by email to the list.

05

Choosing the winners

This is not a draw. After launch we pick the 10 testers whose feedback helped us most, judged on how clear, specific and useful it was. Our decision is final. If fewer than 10 testers send feedback, there are fewer winners.

06

Prize

Each winner gets one App Store offer code that unlocks Sticta Unlimited for free (normal price $9.99). The code is for the winner's own use, cannot be exchanged for cash, and has to be redeemed before the expiry date Apple sets for it.

07

Telling the winners

We email each winner at the address they gave us with their feedback, within 14 days of launch. If a winner doesn't reply or redeem the code within 30 days, we may choose another tester.

08

Your data

We use the email address you give us with your feedback only to contact you about the giveaway and your prize, as described in the privacy policy. Taking part doesn't add you to the Sticta email list. TestFlight is run by Apple under its own terms.

09

Changes

If the beta doesn't go ahead or has to stop, we may change or cancel the giveaway and will tell the list by email. Nothing here limits your rights under the law of your country.
