Beta giveaway rules

In short: the 10 beta testers who send the most useful feedback get Sticta Unlimited free.

01

Organiser

The giveaway is run by Mateusz Czechowski, the developer of Sticta (“we”). Contact: sticta@proton.me. Apple is not a sponsor of this giveaway and is not involved in it in any way.

02

Who can take part

Anyone aged 18 or over who joins the Sticta TestFlight beta through the Sticta email list. Beta places are limited and handed out by us. Taking part is free: no purchase, rating, review, share or follow is needed, and none of these improves your chances.

03

How to take part

Send us feedback about Sticta while the beta runs, through the TestFlight feedback button or by email to sticta@proton.me. Bug reports, things that confused you, ideas and wallpapers you made all count. You can send as much feedback as you like.

04

When

From the day the beta opens until the day Sticta launches on the Mac App Store. We announce both dates by email to the list.

05

Choosing the winners

This is not a draw. After launch we pick the 10 testers whose feedback helped us most, judged on how clear, specific and useful it was. Our decision is final. If fewer than 10 testers send feedback, there are fewer winners.

06

Prize

Each winner gets one App Store offer code that unlocks Sticta Unlimited for free (normal price $9.99). The code is for the winner's own use, cannot be exchanged for cash, and has to be redeemed before the expiry date Apple sets for it.

07

Telling the winners

We email each winner at the address they joined the list with, within 14 days of launch. If a winner doesn't reply or redeem the code within 30 days, we may choose another tester.

08

Your data

We only use the email address you already gave us for the Sticta email list, as described in the privacy policy. TestFlight feedback is handled by Apple under its own terms.

09

Changes

If the beta doesn't go ahead or has to stop, we may change or cancel the giveaway and will tell the list by email. Nothing here limits your rights under the law of your country.
