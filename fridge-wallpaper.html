How to make a fridge photo wallpaper for your Mac

In short: Choose the Fridge style on the Canvas, drop in your photos and each one is held on with a magnet. Set it as wallpaper and your desktop looks like the front of a fridge.

01

What you end up with

A fridge door as your desktop, with photos stuck on by round magnets: the family, the dog, the friends from last summer. It is the corner of the kitchen you look at every day, only on your Mac.

02

Make it in Sticta

  1. Create a project and open the Canvas.
  2. Pick the Fridge style, then a body colour: White, Steel, Black, or any colour with Body color.
  3. Drag your photos in. Each sticker gets a magnet.
  4. Drag a sticker near a slot on the grid to snap it into a tidy row. Drag it away from every slot to leave it free, like a magnet put on crooked.
  5. Click Set as Wallpaper.
03

Make it look like real prints

Want a different look? Select a sticker and pick a style in the inspector: Classic (cut-out with an outline), Polaroid, Original photo (the photo as it was taken, no cut-out) or Clean cut. Mixing styles makes a wallpaper feel less uniform.

On the fridge, Original photo works best for the rectangular prints, and Classic for people and pets you want as cut-outs. The example uses both.

04

Make room for the desktop

The editor shows where the menu bar, the Dock and desktop icons will sit, so no magnet ends up hidden behind an icon.

05

Private by design

Sticta never connects to the internet and your photos never leave your Mac. Privacy policy.
