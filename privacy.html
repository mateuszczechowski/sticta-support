Sticta Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026

In short: Sticta does not collect any data. Your photos, stickers and projects stay on your Mac. The app doesn't connect to the internet, has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

This policy explains how Sticta for macOS (“Sticta”, “the app”) handles your information. Sticta is developed by Mateusz Czechowski (“we”, “us”).

Data we collect

None. We don't collect, receive, store or share any personal data, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers. We have no servers that the app talks to.

Your photos

Sticta has no access to your Photos library. You choose each photo yourself, using the standard macOS file picker or by dragging it into the app. Sticta only sees the photos you give it.

To make stickers, Sticta keeps a resized copy of each chosen photo together with the cut-out mask you create. To place stickers on the travel map, Sticta reads the location (GPS coordinates) stored in the photo and works out the country entirely on your Mac, using map data built into the app. Location data never leaves your device.

Where your data is stored

Projects, stickers and generated wallpaper images are stored locally in Sticta's own folder on your Mac. They aren't sent to us or to anyone else. If you use iCloud or Time Machine to back up your Mac, those copies are handled by Apple under Apple's terms, not by us.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual stickers and projects inside the app at any time. Deleting Sticta from your Mac removes the app and its data folder. Because we never hold a copy of your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Purchases

Sticta is distributed through the Mac App Store. Any purchase is processed by Apple. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Sticta doesn't collect data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Since we don't hold any personal data, there is nothing to access or delete. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we delete the conversation when it's no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

If Sticta ever changes how it handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact
