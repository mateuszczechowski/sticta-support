Privacy Policy

In short: Sticta doesn't collect any data. Your photos, stickers and projects stay on your Mac.

This policy explains how Sticta for macOS (“Sticta”, “the app”) handles your information. Sticta is made by Mateusz Czechowski (“we”, “us”).

01

Data we collect

None. We don't collect, receive, store or share any personal data, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers. We don't run any servers that the app talks to.

02

Your photos

Sticta never browses your Photos library. You choose each photo yourself, using the standard macOS file picker or by dragging it into the app. Sticta only sees the photos you give it.

To make stickers, Sticta keeps a resized copy of each photo you choose, together with the cut-out you create. Sticta reads the location (GPS coordinates) and the date stored in the photo to place stickers, and works out the country on your Mac, using map data built into the app. Location and dates never leave your Mac.

If you choose to save your wallpapers to Photos, macOS asks for your permission first. Sticta then only adds its own pictures to a “Sticta” album and, if you ask it to, replaces the earlier versions it added there. It does not read or change any other photos. iCloud Photos may sync that album to your other devices under Apple’s terms.

03

Where your data is stored

Projects, stickers and the wallpaper images Sticta creates are stored in Sticta's own folder on your Mac. They are never sent to us or anyone else. If you back up your Mac with iCloud or Time Machine, Apple handles those copies under its own terms, not us.

04

Deleting your data

You can delete stickers and projects in the app at any time. Deleting Sticta from your Mac removes the app and its data folder. We never have a copy of your data, so there's nothing for us to delete.

05

Purchases

Sticta is free to download from the Mac App Store and offers one optional in-app purchase, Sticta Unlimited. Apple processes all purchases, including restoring a purchase and Family Sharing. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account.

To know whether Sticta Unlimited is unlocked, the app asks macOS (Apple's StoreKit), which checks the purchase on your Mac. Sticta itself sends nothing anywhere. It only remembers on your Mac whether the purchase is active, so the app opens unlocked straight away. Apple's Privacy Policy explains how Apple handles purchase information.

06

Children

Sticta doesn't collect data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

07

This website, our email list and the beta

This website (sticta.app) sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. To see which pages are visited, it uses a simple analytics service, Page Pulse, which runs on Supabase servers. When you open a page it receives the page address, the page you came from, your browser type and screen width, your IP address (as part of any web request), which links and buttons you click, how long the page stays open and how fast it loads. It has no persistent visitor ID and no link to your identity, so we can't recognise you from one visit to the next. We use it only to improve the site. The Sticta app itself still has no analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. We don't see those logs.

If you join Sticta's email list, your address is stored by our email provider, EmailOctopus (Three Hearts Digital Ltd, United Kingdom), on servers in Ireland. EmailOctopus handles it only on our behalf. We use it only to email you about Sticta: first a confirmation email, and after that occasional news such as new scenes or offers. People who joined before launch also got news about the beta and the launch announcement with a discount code. If you came through one of our links that carries a source tag (such as ?ref=reddit), we store that tag with your address, so we know which posts bring people in. We never sell or share your address. You can unsubscribe with the link in any of these emails, or write to us and we'll delete your address straight away. We keep it until you unsubscribe.

If you test the Sticta beta through Apple's TestFlight, we receive only the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send, together with the details TestFlight attaches to them, such as your Mac model and macOS version. We use them only to improve Sticta and, if you give us your email address, to reply to you and to contact the winners of the beta giveaway. We delete them once the beta and the giveaway are over. Apple runs TestFlight under its own privacy policy.

08

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Apart from an email address you gave us for Sticta's email list and any beta feedback you sent us (see above), we don't hold any personal data about you. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and we delete the conversation once it's no longer needed.

09

Changes to this policy

If Sticta ever changes how it handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
